Common Pitfalls: Avoiding Misinterpretations of Tone and Intent

• Avoid labeling the father as 'angry'; use 'melancholic' or 'wistful' instead.
• Do not view the son's silence as intentional malice; it is a communication gap.
• The poem is a study of grief and longing, not a story of active resentment.
• The ending is an 'unresolved stalemate,' not a definitive permanent break.
• Always link literary devices to the underlying emotional intent in your answers.
