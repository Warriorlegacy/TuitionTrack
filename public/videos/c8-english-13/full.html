• Rhyme: The repetition of similar sounds at the ends of lines (End Rhyme).
• Internal Rhyme: When rhyming words appear within the same line.
• Rhythm: The 'heartbeat' created by stressed and unstressed syllables.
• Imagery: Using descriptive language to create vivid mental pictures.

Common Mistakes: Misinterpreting Metaphors and Contextual Errors

• Don't take metaphors literally: Macavity is a cat, not a human leader.
• Understand Hyperbole: 'Defying gravity' means agility, not actual magic.
• Use precise vocabulary: Replace 'bad' with 'deceptive' or 'elusive'.
• Balance the duality: Analyze both his cat traits and his human-like intellect.
• Contextualize 'Mystery': It refers to his untraceable methods, not just identity.
