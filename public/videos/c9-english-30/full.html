Common Pitfalls: Misinterpretations and Frequent Student Errors

• Don't confuse 'accidental' with 'careless' or 'irresponsible'.
• Use 'irony' instead of just 'humor' to describe the tone.
• Avoid extreme emotional labels; use 'perplexed' or 'disconcerted'.
• Connect travel experiences to the theme of unpredictability.
• Always anchor your answers in textual evidence, not personal opinion.
