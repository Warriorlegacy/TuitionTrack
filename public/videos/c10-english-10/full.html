Literary Devices I: Alliteration, Enjambment, and Rhyme Scheme

• Alliteration: Repetition of initial consonant sounds (e.g., 'Has... heart')
• Enjambment: A sentence continuing across line breaks without punctuation
• Enjambment mimics the seamless, sudden shift in the poet's mood
• Rhyme Scheme: The pattern of end-rhymes (A-B-A-B, C-D-C-B)
• The 'B' rhyme connects the stanzas, linking action to emotion
