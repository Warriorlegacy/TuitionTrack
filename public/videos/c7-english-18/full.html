Grammar Integration: Adjectives and Adverbs in Descriptive Writing

• Adjectives: Words that describe or modify nouns and pronouns.
• Adverbs: Words that describe verbs, adjectives, or other adverbs.
• Adjectives answer: What kind? Which one? How many?
• Adverbs answer: How? When? Where? To what extent?
• Formula: Use Adjectives for scenery and Adverbs for action.
