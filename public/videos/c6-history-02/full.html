Stone Tool Evolution: Classification of Tools and Manufacturing Techniques

• Paleolithic: Large, crude tools like hand-axes made of quartzite.
• Mesolithic: Small, sharp 'Microliths' used as composite tools.
• Neolithic: Polished, smooth-edged tools designed for farming.
• Flaking Technique: Striking a core with a hammerstone to create flakes.
• Pressure Flaking: Using bone or wood to press precise edges onto stone.
