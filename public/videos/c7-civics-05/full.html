Defining Inequality: Concepts of Gender Bias, Discrimination, and Prejudice

• Prejudice: Pre-judging someone based on preconceived notions or attitudes.
• Gender Bias: A preference or tendency to favor one gender over another.
• Discrimination: The actual unfair treatment or action based on gender.
• The Chain: Prejudice (Thought) -> Bias (Inclination) -> Discrimination (Action).
• Identifying these concepts is the first step toward achieving social equality.
