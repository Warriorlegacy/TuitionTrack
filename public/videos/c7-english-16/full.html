Grammar Integration: Identifying Tenses and Sentence Structures used in Narrative

• Simple Past Tense (V2) is used to report completed narrative actions.
• Past Continuous (Was/Were + Verb-ing) builds the background atmosphere.
• Simple Sentences create sudden impact and dramatic tension.
• Compound Sentences use FANBOYS to connect two equal ideas.
• Complex Sentences use subordinating conjunctions to show relationships.
