Part 3: Operations on Sets – Union, Intersection, Difference, Complement & De Morgan’s Law

• Union (A ∪ B): Elements in A, B, or both.
• Intersection (A ∩ B): Elements common to both A and B.
• Difference (A - B): Elements in A but not in B.
• Complement (A'): Elements in U but not in A.
• De Morgan's Laws: Relating complements with union/intersection.

Part 5: Cartesian Product – Definition, Examples, Properties & Relation to Functions

• A × B = {(a,b) : a ∈ A, b ∈ B} — all ordered pairs
• |A × B| = |A| × |B| — size is product of sizes
• Cartesian product is NOT commutative: A×B ≠ B×A
• Distributes over union & intersection: A×(B∪C)=(A×B)∪(A×C)
• Functions are special subsets of A × B

Part 9: Composition & Inverse Functions – How to Compose, Invert, and When Inverses Exist

• Composition f∘g: apply g first, then f; order matters always.
• Inverse f⁻¹ undoes f; exists only when f is bijective.
• Verify by checking f∘f⁻¹ = I and f⁻¹∘f = I.
• Inverse of composition reverses order: (f∘g)⁻¹ = g⁻¹∘f⁻¹.
• Composition is associative but generally not commutative.
