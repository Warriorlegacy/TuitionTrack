Critical Thinking: Interpreting the Poet's Tone and Mood

• Tone: The poet's attitude toward the subject (e.g., resilient, defiant).
• Mood: The atmosphere or emotion felt by the reader (e.g., awe, hope).
• Clue Words: Use verbs and adjectives to identify emotional shifts.
• Avoid Simplistic Labels: Use precise terms like 'triumphant' instead of 'happy'.
• Critical Tip: Connect the poet's voice to the emotional impact on the reader.

Exam Technique: How to Answer RTC (Reference to Context) Questions

• Step 1: Always identify the Poem and Poet with full names.
• Step 2: Explain the 'Context' by linking literal lines to deeper meaning.
• Step 3: Use precise, academic language; avoid 'I think' or 'I feel'.
• Formula: Statement + Reason = High Scoring Answers.
• Tip: Use keywords like 'symbolizes', 'implies', and 'represents'.
