Part 3 of 12 · ~4 min

Defining Semi-perimeter (s) and the Statement of Heron's Formula

• Semi-perimeter (s) is half of the total perimeter
• Formula for semi-perimeter: s = (a + b + c) / 2
• Heron's Formula: Area = square root of [s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)]
• Works for any triangle if all three sides are known
• Final area is always expressed in square units

Step-by-Step Derivation and Conceptual Understanding of the Formula

• Derivation is based on the Pythagorean Theorem
• Height divides the triangle into two right-angled triangles
• Algebraic substitution eliminates height and segment x
• The formula links side lengths directly to area
• If s equals a, b, or c, the area becomes zero

Advanced Applications: Finding Area of Quadrilaterals using Triangulation

• Quadrilaterals can be split into two triangles using a diagonal.
• Total Area = Area of Triangle 1 + Area of Triangle 2.
• Use Heron's Formula separately for each triangle.
• The diagonal acts as a common side for both triangles.
• This triangulation method works for any polygon shape.
