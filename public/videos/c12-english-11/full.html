Common Mistakes: Misinterpreting Metaphors and Contextual Errors

• Avoid literal interpretations: 'Bower' is a mental sanctuary, not a physical hut.
• Understand 'Pall': It signifies an oppressive, death-like weight of sadness.
• The 'Endless Fountain' is spiritual nourishment, not a literal mythical drink.
• Connect natural elements like the Sun to the theme of cosmic permanence.
• Avoid the 'Literalism Trap': Always link imagery to its underlying emotion.
