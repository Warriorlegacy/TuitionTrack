Increasing and Decreasing Functions: Concept, Intervals, and Derivative Tests

• Increasing: If x1 < x2 implies f(x1) <= f(x2)
• Decreasing: If x1 < x2 implies f(x1) >= f(x2)
• Strictly Increasing: f'(x) > 0 for all x in the interval
• Strictly Decreasing: f'(x) < 0 for all x in the interval
• Critical Points: Where f'(x) = 0 or is undefined

Tangents and Normals: Geometric Interpretation, Slope Formulas, and Equations

• Tangent: A line that touches the curve at a point, mimicking its local direction.
• Slope of Tangent (m-t): The value of the derivative f prime of a at point a.
• Equation of Tangent: y minus y-one equals m-t times x minus x-one.
• Normal: A line perpendicular to the tangent at the point of contact.
• Slope of Normal (m-n): The negative reciprocal of the tangent slope, -1 divided by m-t.

Tangents and Normals: Advanced Applications and Graphical Analysis

• Angle between two curves: tan(theta) = |m1 - m2| / (1 + m1*m2)
• Length of Tangent = |y / m|; Length of Sub-tangent = |y / m|
• Length of Normal = |y * sqrt(1 + m^2)|; Length of Sub-normal = |y * m|
• Vertical tangents occur when the derivative is undefined (approaches infinity)
• The rotation of the tangent line determines the curvature and concavity

Maxima and Minima: Local and Absolute Extrema, Critical Points, and First Derivative Test

• Absolute Extrema: The highest or lowest values over an entire interval.
• Local Extrema: Highest or lowest values within a specific neighborhood.
• Critical Points: Points where f prime of x is zero or undefined.
• First Derivative Test: Using sign changes of f prime to identify extrema.
• Positive to Negative sign change indicates a Local Maximum.

Part 8 of 12 · ~4 min

Maxima and Minima: Second Derivative Test, Derivations, and Visualizing Concavity

• Second Derivative Test: A faster way to classify critical points.
• If f double prime of c is negative, the point is a local maximum.
• If f double prime of c is positive, the point is a local minimum.
• Concave Up: Curve bends upward; tangents lie below the graph.
• Concave Down: Curve bends downward; tangents lie above the graph.
