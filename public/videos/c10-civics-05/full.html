Accountable, Responsive, and Legitimate Government: Definitions and Core Concepts

• Accountability: The government's obligation to be answerable to citizens.
• Responsiveness: Sensitivity to the needs, demands, and expectations of the public.
• Legitimacy: The legal authority derived from the consent of the governed.
• Accountability is maintained through elections, free press, and transparency.
• Legitimacy ensures political stability even during periods of slow decision-making.
