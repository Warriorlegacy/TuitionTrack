The Legal and Social Landscape of Champaran in 1917

• The Tinkathia System: A legal trap forcing indigo cultivation on 15% of land.
• Legalized Extortion: Laws were used as tools for British planters to exploit peasants.
• The Three-Tier Social Hierarchy: British planters, Indian intermediaries, and peasants.
• A Culture of Fear: Peasants faced social, economic, and legal marginalization.
• Lack of Social Cohesion: Isolated communities lacked the strength to resist authority.
