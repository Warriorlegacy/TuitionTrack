Literary Devices I: Alliteration, Epithet, and Personification

• Alliteration: Repetition of consonant sounds at the start of nearby words.
• The 't' sound in 'terribly transient' emphasizes the fleeting nature of life.
• Epithet: An adjective used to describe a characteristic quality of a noun.
• Personification: Giving human traits or agency to non-human elements like the sea.
• These devices transform simple descriptions into profound philosophical truths.
