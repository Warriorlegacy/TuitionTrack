The Strange Case of Britain: The Unification of the United Kingdom

• Britain's unification was a long-drawn-out political process, not a sudden revolution.
• Before the 18th century, no nation-state existed; people had diverse identities.
• The Act of Union 1707 allowed England to incorporate Scotland into Great Britain.
• Ireland was forcibly incorporated into the United Kingdom in 1801 after a revolt.
• National identity was built through symbols like the Union Jack and the national anthem.
