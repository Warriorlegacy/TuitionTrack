Poetic Devices I: Similes, Metaphors, and Personification in the Poem

• Simile: Comparing two things using 'like' or 'as'.
• Metaphor: A direct comparison stating one thing IS another.
• Personification: Giving human qualities to animals or objects.
• Similes create vivid imagery through explicit comparison.
• Metaphors and Personification deepen the poem's emotional impact.

Poetic Devices II: Alliteration, Rhyme Scheme, and Rhythm Analysis

• Alliteration: Repetition of initial consonant sounds to create mood.
• Rhyme Scheme: The pattern of end-rhymes labeled with letters (e.g., AABB).
• Rhyming Couplets: Two consecutive lines that rhyme with each other.
• Rhythm: The musical beat created by stressed and unstressed syllables.
• Analysis Tip: Connect poetic devices to the emotions they evoke.
