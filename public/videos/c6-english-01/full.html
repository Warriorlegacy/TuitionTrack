Grammar Focus: Tenses and Sentence Structures in the Story

• Simple Past: Subject + V2 (e.g., searched) for completed actions.
• Past Continuous: Subject + was/were + V-ing for ongoing past actions.
• Past Perfect: Subject + had + V3 for actions completed before another past event.
• Sentence Types: Simple, Compound (using conjunctions), and Complex.
• Using Complex Sentences to show cause, effect, and timing in narratives.
