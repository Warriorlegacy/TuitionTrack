Grammar Focus: Adjectives and Adverbs in Descriptive Writing

• Adjectives describe nouns (What kind? Which one?)
• Adverbs describe verbs (How? When? Where?)
• Formula: Adjective + Noun (e.g., The cunning fox)
• Formula: Verb + Adverb (e.g., He spoke sweetly)
• Use precise words to create vivid imagery
