Literary Devices I: Metaphor, Simile, and Personification

• Simile: Comparing two things using 'like' or 'as'
• Metaphor: A direct comparison stating one thing IS another
• Personification: Giving human qualities to non-human things
• Metaphorical Depth: Using physical objects to represent emotions
• The Role of Devices: Moving from literal meaning to poetic feeling

Literary Devices II: Alliteration, Assonance, and Onomatopoeia

• Alliteration: Repetition of initial consonant sounds (e.g., 'lake water lapping low')
• Assonance: Repetition of vowel sounds within words to create internal rhythm
• Onomatopoeia: Words that imitate the natural sounds they describe
• How 'L' sounds in Alliteration mimic the gentle flow of water
• How Onomatopoeia like 'lapping' brings sensory realism to the poem
