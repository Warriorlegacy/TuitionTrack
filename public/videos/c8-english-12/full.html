Common Mistakes: Misinterpreting Symbolism and Structural Errors

• Symbolism: Height represents perspective, not just physical altitude.
• Nuance: The poet is disillusioned by borders, not confused by them.
• Avoid the Summary Trap: Analyze the 'why', don't just retell the 'what'.
• Grammar Tip: Always use the Literary Present Tense for analysis.
• Precision: Avoid vague pronouns; specify exactly what 'this' or 'it' refers to.
