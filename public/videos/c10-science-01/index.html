Topic 1 · Chemical Equations and Balancing

Chemical Equations and Balancing

A chemical equation is a shorthand way to represent a reaction using symbols. It is like a recipe where the ingredients (reactants) must exactly equal the finished dish (products) to satisfy the Law of Conservation of Ma

• Reactants are on the left, products on the right.
• Use coefficients to balance, never change subscripts.
• State symbols: (s) solid, (l) liquid, (g) gas, (aq) aqueous.

H2 + O2 -> H2O becomes 2H2 + O2 -> 2H2O.

Topic 2 · Combination and Decomposition Reactions

Combination and Decomposition Reactions

Combination is when two elements join to form one product, like two people hugging. Decomposition is the opposite, where one compound breaks into two or more parts, like a single Lego structure being pulled apart.

• Combination: A + B -> AB
• Decomposition: AB -> A + B
• Thermal decomposition requires heat energy.

CaO + H2O -> Ca(OH)2 (Combination).

Topic 3 · Displacement and Double Displacement Reactions

Displacement and Double Displacement Reactions

In displacement, a stronger element kicks out a weaker one from its place, like a star player replacing a bench player. In double displacement, two compounds exchange partners to form new ones.

• Displacement: A + BC -> AC + B
• Double Displacement: AB + CD -> AD + CB
• Often involves the formation of a precipitate (insoluble solid).

Fe + CuSO4 -> FeSO4 + Cu (Displacement).

Topic 4 · Oxidation and Reduction (Redox)

Oxidation and Reduction (Redox)

Oxidation is the gain of oxygen or loss of electrons, while reduction is the loss of oxygen or gain of electrons. Think of it as a dance where one person gives a prop (oxygen) and the other receives it.

• Oxidizing agent: The substance that gives oxygen.
• Reducing agent: The substance that takes oxygen.
• Redox occurs when both processes happen simultaneously.

CuO + H2 -> Cu + H2O (CuO is reduced, H2 is oxidized).

Topic 5 · Corrosion and Rancidity

Corrosion and Rancidity

Corrosion is the slow eating away of metals by air and moisture, like rust on an old gate. Rancidity is the oxidation of fats and oils in food, making them smell bad, similar to how an apple turns brown.

• Rusting formula: 4Fe + 3O2 + xH2O -> 2Fe2O3.xH2O
• Prevent corrosion via galvanization or painting.
• Prevent rancidity by using nitrogen gas in food packets.

Silver turning black due to silver sulphide formation.
