Literary Devices I: Alliteration, Assonance, and Consonance in the Poem

• Alliteration: Repetition of consonant sounds at the start of nearby words.
• Assonance: Repetition of similar vowel sounds within words.
• Consonance: Repetition of consonant sounds anywhere in the words.
• Sound devices create rhythm, mood, and musicality in poetry.
• These tools help mimic natural elements like the sea or wind.

Literary Devices II: Personification, Simile, and Hyperbole

• Personification: Giving human traits to non-human elements like nature.
• Simile: Comparing two distinct things using the words 'like' or 'as'.
• Hyperbole: Using extreme exaggeration to emphasize intense emotions.
• Purpose: These devices transform abstract feelings into vivid imagery.
• Exam Tip: Always explain 'why' a device is used, not just 'what' it is.
