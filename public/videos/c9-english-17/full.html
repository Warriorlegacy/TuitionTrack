Common Mistakes: Avoiding Misinterpretations of Symbolism and Tone

• Don't limit 'uniforms' to just military clothing; it symbolizes all superficial identities.
• Avoid calling the tone just 'peaceful'; use 'urgent', 'didactic', or 'cautionary'.
• The Earth is a symbol of shared existence, not just a physical setting.
• War is described as self-betrayal, not just an attack on others.
• Always distinguish between literal descriptions and metaphorical meanings.
