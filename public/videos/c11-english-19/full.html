Common Pitfalls: Avoiding Misinterpretations of the Satire

• Don't mistake the King's character for simple stupidity; focus on arbitrary power.
• Avoid seeing the crowd as heroes; they represent fickle, mindless public apathy.
• The Melon's rule is not a 'happy ending' but a critique of political indifference.
• The Chain of Blame is a systemic critique of accountability, not just a joke.
• Always connect the poem's absurdity to serious themes of governance and duty.
