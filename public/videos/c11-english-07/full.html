The Physical Toll: Understanding Altitude Sickness and Physiological Challenges

• Atmospheric pressure drops at high altitudes, reducing oxygen density.
• Hypoxia: The condition where tissues are starved of adequate oxygen.
• Symptoms of AMS include throbbing headaches, nausea, and extreme fatigue.
• Hyperventilation and periodic breathing affect sleep and oxygen intake.
• The body uses extra energy to maintain temperature and basic functions.
