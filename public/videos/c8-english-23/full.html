Common Pitfalls: Avoiding Mistakes in Character Interpretation and Summary Writing

• Avoid labeling the Princess as 'cruel'; use 'possessive' or 'insecure' instead.
• Distinguish between the bird's 'instinct for freedom' and 'rebellion'.
• Avoid the 'Detail Trap': Focus on plot arc, not descriptive imagery.
• Maintain objectivity: Do not inject personal opinions into your summary.
• Use consistent Simple Present Tense when writing summaries.
