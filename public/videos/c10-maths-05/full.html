Defining Arithmetic Progression: First Term, Common Difference, and General Form

• Arithmetic Progression: A sequence with a constant difference between terms.
• First Term (a): The starting value of the sequence.
• Common Difference (d): The constant value added to each term to get the next.
• The common difference 'd' can be positive, negative, or zero.
• General Form: a, a + d, a + 2d, a + 3d, ...

The nth Term Formula: Derivation and Step-by-Step Application

• Identify first term (a) and common difference (d)
• Derivation: a_n = a + (n - 1)d
• The multiplier for 'd' is always one less than the term position
• Step-by-step: Identify variables, Substitute, Simplify, Solve
• Works for finding any term, 'a', 'd', or the position 'n'

Finding Specific Terms and Missing Variables in an AP

• Use a = first term, d = common difference, and n = position of the term
• Formula: nth term = a + (n - 1)d
• To find specific terms, substitute the known values into the nth term formula
• To find missing variables, create two equations using the nth term formula
• Solve the resulting simultaneous equations to find 'a' and 'd'

Common Pitfalls: Sign Errors and Misinterpreting Common Difference

• Always use parentheses when substituting a negative common difference (d).
• Watch for keywords like 'decreasing' or 'falling' to identify negative 'd'.
• Common difference is strictly the difference between consecutive terms.
• Subtracting the first term from the third term gives 2d, not d.
• Perform a 'trend check': Does your answer match the sequence direction?

Part 6 of 12 · ~5 min

Sum of First n Terms: Derivation of the Formula

• S sub n represents the sum of the first n terms of an AP
• Write the sum in forward order and then in reverse order
• Adding the two equations creates n identical pairs
• Each pair sums to: 2a plus (n minus 1)d
• Final Formula: S sub n equals n/2 [2a plus (n - 1)d]

Part 7 of 12 · ~5 min

Sum of First n Terms: Alternative Formula and Application Scenarios

• Standard Formula: S_n = 1/2 * n * [2a + (n-1)d]
• Alternative Formula: S_n = 1/2 * n * (a + l)
• Use 'l' formula when the last term is known
• Saves time and reduces calculation errors
• Identify given variables (a, d, n, or l) before choosing

Arithmetic Mean and Relationship between Terms

• Definition: If a, b, c are in AP, then b = (a + c) / 2
• The middle term is the Arithmetic Mean of its neighbors
• Pro-tip: Use (a-d), a, (a+d) for three terms in AP to simplify sums
• Relationship: If m-th term is n and n-th term is m, then d = -1
• The arithmetic mean provides the balance point of a sequence
