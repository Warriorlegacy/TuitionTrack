Introduction: Real-world Applications, Importance, and the Concept of Angles

• Trigonometry models waves, cycles, and rotations in nature.
• Applications include astronomy, engineering, and signal processing.
• An angle represents the amount of rotation between two rays.
• Degrees: Based on a 360-degree full rotation.
• Radians: Based on the relationship between arc length and radius.

Angles and Their Measurement: Degree vs. Radian Measure and Conversions

• Degree Measure: 1 degree = 60 minutes; 1 minute = 60 seconds
• Radian Measure: Angle subtended by an arc equal to the radius
• Conversion: Degrees to Radians = Multiply by (pi / 180)
• Conversion: Radians to Degrees = Multiply by (180 / pi)
• Relationship: 360 degrees = 2 pi radians

Trigonometric Functions: Unit Circle, Signs in Quadrants, and Domain/Range

• Unit Circle: Center (0,0), Radius = 1 unit
• On Unit Circle: x = cos(theta) and y = sin(theta)
• Quadrant Signs: All (Q1), Sine (Q2), Tan (Q3), Cos (Q4)
• Sine and Cosine Domain: All Real Numbers (R)
• Sine and Cosine Range: Interval [-1, 1]

Trigonometric Functions: Graphs of Sine, Cosine, Tangent, Cosecant, Secant, and Cotangent

• Sine and Cosine are continuous waves with a range of negative one to one.
• Tangent and Cotangent have vertical asymptotes where the denominator is zero.
• Sine and Cosine graphs are horizontal shifts of each other.
• Cosecant and Secant are reciprocal curves that touch the sine and cosine peaks.
• Domain and range determine the boundaries and existence of each graph.

Sign of Trigonometric Functions and Periodicity: Detailed Analysis

• Quadrant I: All functions are positive
• Quadrant II: Sine and Cosecant are positive
• Quadrant III: Tangent and Cotangent are positive
• Quadrant IV: Cosine and Secant are positive
• Periodicity: Sine/Cosine period is 2 pi; Tangent/Cotangent period is pi

Trigonometric Functions: Fundamental Identities and Compound Angle Formulas

• Pythagorean Identities: sin²x + cos²x = 1, tan²x + 1 = sec²x, 1 + cot²x = cosec²x
• Sine Compound Angles: sin(x ± y) = sin x cos y ± cos x sin y
• Cosine Compound Angles: cos(x ± y) = cos x cos y ∓ sin x sin y
• Tangent Compound Angles: tan(x ± y) = (tan x ± tan y) / (1 ∓ tan x tan y)
• Tip: Observe the sign changes and function mixing patterns for easier recall

Sum and Difference Formulas: Derivations and Worked Examples

• Cosine Sum/Difference: Sign flips between cosine and sine terms
• Sine Sum/Difference: Sine and cosine terms are mixed
• Tangent Sum/Difference: Uses a fractional structure with products

Trigonometric Equations: General and Principal Solutions

• General Solutions: Formulas representing infinite solutions using integer 'n'
