Common Mistakes: Avoiding Errors in Character Motivation and Plot Sequencing

• Avoid labeling Griffin as a hero; he is explicitly a 'lawless person'.
• Distinguish between 'survival' and 'intentional lawlessness' in his motives.
• Griffin's rudeness was a tactic for secrecy, not a sign of shyness.
• Maintain strict chronological order: London chaos leads to Iping retreat.
• Correct Cause and Effect: Science enabled the crime; crime did not cause science.
