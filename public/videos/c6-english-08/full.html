Poetic Devices: Rhyme Scheme, Imagery, and Personification

• Rhyme Scheme: The pattern of sounds at the end of lines (e.g., A-B-B-C).
• Imagery: Using descriptive words to create mental pictures through senses.
• Visual Imagery: Words that help you 'see' colors, shapes, and sizes.
• Tactile Imagery: Words that help you 'feel' textures and temperatures.
• Personification: Giving human thoughts and feelings to animals or objects.

Common Mistakes: Misinterpreting Metaphors and Tense Errors

• Avoid literal interpretations: Metaphors represent ideas, not just objects.
• Use the Literary Present Tense when analyzing the poem's meaning.
• Watch out for Tense Shifting: Keep your sentence structure consistent.
• Metaphor Tip: The shell represents a limited, safe world.
• Grammar Tip: Use strong verbs like 'evolves' or 'represents' instead of 'is'.
