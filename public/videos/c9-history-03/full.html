The Birth of Weimar Republic: Treaty of Versailles and the Impact of War

• The Weimar Republic was established in the town of Weimar to avoid unrest.
• Treaty of Versailles: A 'Diktat' or dictated peace that humiliated Germany.
• Territorial Losses: Alsace-Lorraine returned to France; overseas colonies lost.
• Military Restrictions: Army limited to 100,000 men; no air force or submarines.
• War Guilt Clause: Article 231 forced Germany to accept total war responsibility.

Economic Crisis in Germany: Hyperinflation, the Great Depression, and Political Instability

• French occupation of the Ruhr triggered massive money printing.
• Hyperinflation: The German Mark lost nearly all its value.
• The Great Depression: US loans stopped after the 1929 Wall Street Crash.
• Unemployment hit 6 million, causing widespread social despair.
• Political instability: Proportional representation led to weak governments.

Common Pitfalls: Distinguishing between Political Ideology and Historical Fact

• Political Ideology: A set of beliefs used to justify actions or visions.
• Historical Fact: Objective, verifiable realities proven by evidence.
• Avoid treating subjective Nazi beliefs as objective biological truths.
• Distinguish between what a group 'intended' and what 'actually happened'.
• Analyze propaganda as ideology and records as historical facts.
