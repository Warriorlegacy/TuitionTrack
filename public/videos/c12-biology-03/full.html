Amniocentesis: The Science, Procedure, and Ethical/Legal Implications

• Amniotic fluid contains fetal cells used for genetic testing.
• Karyotyping detects chromosomal abnormalities like Down Syndrome.
• Procedure involves ultrasound-guided needle aspiration of fluid.
• Misuse for sex determination leads to skewed sex ratios.
• PCPNDT Act strictly prohibits sex determination via amniocentesis.

Contraceptive Methods II: IUDs, Oral Pills, and Injectables/Implants

• IUDs: Non-medicated, Copper-releasing, and Hormone-releasing types.
• Copper IUDs (Cu-T) suppress sperm motility and fertilizing capacity.
• Hormone-releasing IUDs (LNG-20) prevent implantation.
• Oral Pills: Inhibit ovulation using progestogens or estrogen.
• Saheli: A non-steroidal, once-a-week pill developed in India.

Part 7 of 12 · ~3 min

Contraceptive Methods III: Surgical Methods and Emergency Contraception

• Surgical methods are terminal and highly effective permanent solutions.
• Vasectomy: Small part of the vas deferens is removed or tied.
• Tubectomy: Small part of the fallopian tube is removed or tied.
• Emergency Contraception: Used within 72 hours to delay ovulation.
• Emergency pills prevent pregnancy; they do not terminate existing ones.

Medical Termination of Pregnancy (MTP): Legal Status, Safety, and Medical Guidelines

• MTP is the intentional termination of pregnancy by medical removal or absorption.
• MTP was legalized in India in 1971 to prevent unsafe abortions.
• Legal grounds: Risk to maternal life, mental health, or fetal abnormalities.
• Safety is highest during the first trimester, up to twelve weeks.
• Procedures must be performed by registered doctors in recognized facilities.

Sexually Transmitted Infections (STIs): Classification, Symptoms, and Prevention

• STIs are also known as Venereal Diseases (VD)
• Classification: Bacterial, Viral, and Protozoan pathogens
• Common Symptoms: Itching, discharge, and pain during urination
• Risks: Infertility, pelvic inflammatory disease, and ectopic pregnancy
• Prevention: Avoid multiple partners and use barrier methods like condoms

Infertility: Biological Causes and Diagnostic Approaches

• Infertility: Inability to conceive after 2 years of unprotected intercourse
• Male Causes: Low sperm count, poor motility, and abnormal morphology
• Female Causes: Ovulatory dysfunction, tubal blockage, and uterine issues
• Male Diagnostics: Semen analysis and hormonal assays
• Female Diagnostics: Ultrasound, hormone testing, and Hysterosalpingography

Part 11 of 12 · ~4 min

Assisted Reproductive Technologies (ART) I: IVF, ZIFT, and GIFT

• Assisted Reproductive Technologies (ART) assist in overcoming infertility.
• In Vitro Fertilization (IVF): Fertilization occurs outside the body in a lab.
• Zygote Intra Fallopian Transfer (ZIFT): Early embryo transferred to fallopian tube.
• Gamete Intra Fallopian Transfer (GIFT): Unfertilized egg and sperm transferred to tube.
• ZIFT uses early embryos; GIFT uses unfertilized gametes for natural meeting.
