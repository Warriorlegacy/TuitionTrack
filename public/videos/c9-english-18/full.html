Common Pitfalls: Misinterpreting Tone and Contextual Errors

• Avoid labeling the Duck as 'bossy'; use 'earnest' or 'persuasive' instead.
• Distinguish between the Kangaroo's 'rudeness' and his 'logical reasoning'.
• Recognize the poem as 'Nonsense Verse' rather than a strict moral fable.
• Avoid the 'Literal Interpretation Trap'; focus on symbolism over mechanics.
• Always check the surrounding stanzas to ensure correct tonal context.
