Comprehension Workshop: Step-by-Step Analysis of Textual Questions

• Read the question twice; underline action words like explain or why.
• Find the exact line in the story that gives the answer.
• Write in your own words; never copy whole sentences.
• Check your answer includes who, what, where, and why.
• For true or false, prove your answer with one story line.

Grammar Integration: Identifying Verbs and Adverbs in the Narrative

• Verbs: Words that express action or a state of being.
• Adverbs: Words that describe how, when, or where an action happens.
• The Relationship: Adverbs modify and add detail to verbs.
• Identification Tip: Many adverbs end in the suffix '-ly'.
• Formula: Verb (The Action) + Adverb (The Manner) = Vivid Imagery.

Common Mistakes: Avoiding Errors in Interpretation and Sentence Structure

• Avoid misinterpreting Dad's character: focus on 'overconfidence' over 'clumsiness'.
• Understand the irony: the rescue attempt caused more chaos than the original problem.
• Maintain Tense Consistency: use the Simple Past Tense for narrative descriptions.
• Fix Sentence Fragments: use conjunctions to create smooth, complex sentences.
• Ensure Subject-Verb Agreement: check that singular subjects have singular verbs.
