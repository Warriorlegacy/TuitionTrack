Fundamental Definitions: Tangents, Secants, and the Point of Contact

• Secant: A line that intersects a circle at exactly two distinct points.
• Tangent: A line that touches the circle at only one single point.
• Point of Contact: The unique point where the tangent meets the circle.
• Key Difference: Secants pass through the interior; tangents graze the boundary.
• Etymology: 'Tangent' comes from the Latin 'tangere', meaning 'to touch'.

Theorem 10.1: The Perpendicularity of Radius and Tangent (Proof & Visuals)

• Theorem 10.1: Tangent is perpendicular to the radius at the point of contact.
• Method of Proof: Proof by Contradiction.
• Assumption: Assume radius OP is not perpendicular to tangent line L.
• Contradiction: The hypotenuse must be the longest side in a right triangle.
• Conclusion: The radius must be perpendicular to the tangent line.

Theorem 10.2: Tangents from an External Point (Proof & Geometric Construction)

• Theorem 10.2: Tangents from an external point to a circle are equal in length.
• Construction: Join center O to point P, and to points of contact A and B.
• Proof Method: Use RHS Congruence Rule on triangles OAP and OBP.
• Key Result: PA = PB (Corresponding Parts of Congruent Triangles).
• Symmetry: The line OP bisects the angle between the two tangents.

Exploring Tangent Properties: Lengths, Symmetry, and Congruent Triangles

• Tangents from an external point create two right-angled triangles
• Radii OA and OB are equal, and OP is a common hypotenuse
• Triangles OAP and OBP are congruent by the RHS rule
• CPCT proves tangents PA and PB are equal in length
• Line OP bisects both the angle at the center and the angle at point P

Mathematical Derivations: Using Pythagoras Theorem in Circle Geometry

• Radius is perpendicular to the tangent at the point of contact
• Tangent, Radius, and Center-to-External-Point form a right triangle
• Pythagoras Theorem: (Distance to Center)² = (Radius)² + (Tangent Length)²
• To find Tangent Length: Square root of (OP² - Radius²)
• Congruent triangles allow Pythagoras to be applied to both tangent segments
