Common Mistakes: Misinterpreting Irony and Character Motivations

• Avoid calling the irony 'tragic'; it is 'Situational Irony'.
• Don't label Lencho as 'ungrateful'; explain his 'blind faith'.
• Describe the Postmaster's motive as 'empathy', not 'interference'.
• The core conflict is 'Faith versus Reality', not just 'Man versus Nature'.
• Always link the irony back to the central theme of the story.
