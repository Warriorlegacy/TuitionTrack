Common Pitfalls: Avoiding Errors in Interpretation and Grammar

• Avoid Over-literalism: Distinguish between physical events and metaphors.
• Check Character Emotions: Use textual evidence instead of personal assumptions.
• Maintain Literary Present Tense: Use 'sees' instead of 'saw' for story analysis.
• Master Subject-Verb Agreement: Match verbs to the true subject, not nearby nouns.
• Use Precise Vocabulary: Choose words that match the specific tone of the text.
