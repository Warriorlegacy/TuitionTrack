Basic Terms and Definitions: Points, Lines, Rays, Segments, and Collinearity

• Point: A precise location with no dimensions.
• Line: A straight path extending infinitely in both directions.
• Line Segment: A part of a line with two fixed endpoints.
• Ray: A path with one endpoint extending infinitely in one direction.
• Collinear Points: Points that lie on the same straight line.

Types of Angles: Acute, Right, Obtuse, Straight, Reflex, and Complete Angles

• Acute Angle: 0° < angle < 90°
• Right Angle: Exactly 90°
• Obtuse Angle: 90° < angle < 180°
• Straight Angle: Exactly 180°
• Reflex Angle: 180° < angle < 360°

Adjacent Angles, Linear Pairs, and Vertically Opposite Angles

• Adjacent Angles: Share a common vertex and a common arm
• Linear Pair: Adjacent angles whose non-common arms form a straight line
• Linear Pair Property: Sum of angles is always one hundred and eighty degrees
• Vertically Opposite Angles: Formed by two intersecting lines
• Theorem: Vertically opposite angles are always equal

Pairs of Lines: Intersecting Lines and Parallel Lines with Transversals

• Intersecting lines create Vertically Opposite Angles which are equal
• A Transversal is a line that intersects two or more lines
• Corresponding Angles are in the same relative position and are equal
• Alternate Interior and Exterior Angles are equal when lines are parallel
• Co-interior Angles are supplementary, meaning they sum to 180 degrees

Angle Sum Property of a Triangle: Concept, Derivation, and Proof

• Angle Sum Property: Sum of interior angles of a triangle is 180 degrees.
• Construction: Draw a line through vertex A parallel to side BC.
• Alternate Interior Angles: Angle AXB equals Angle B; Angle AYC equals Angle C.
• Straight Line Property: Angles on a straight line sum to 180 degrees.
• Conclusion: Angle A + Angle B + Angle C = 180 degrees.

Exterior Angle Theorem: Concept, Derivation, and Proof

• Definition: An exterior angle is formed by extending one side of a triangle.
• Theorem: Exterior angle = Sum of the two interior opposite angles.
• Key Property 1: Interior angles of a triangle sum to 180 degrees.
• Key Property 2: Linear pairs on a straight line sum to 180 degrees.
• Proof Logic: Equating the Angle Sum Property and the Linear Pair property.

Part 8 of 12 · ~4 min

Parallel Lines and Transversal: Corresponding, Alternate, and Co-interior Angles

• Corresponding Angles: Located in the same relative position and are equal.
• Alternate Interior Angles: Opposite sides of transversal, inside lines; are equal.
• Alternate Exterior Angles: Opposite sides of transversal, outside lines; are equal.
• Co-interior Angles: Same side of transversal, inside lines; sum to 180 degrees.
• Parallelism Rule: These properties only hold true if the lines are parallel.
