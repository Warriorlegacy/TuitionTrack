The Western Perspective: Physicality, Perspective, and the 'Eye'

• Western art focuses on Mimesis: the imitation of physical reality.
• Emphasis on Physicality: capturing texture, light, weight, and shadow.
• Linear Perspective: using mathematical vanishing points to create depth.
• The 'Window Effect': the viewer acts as an observer outside the scene.
• The 'Eye' as an Objective Observer: prioritizing accuracy over emotion.

The Concept of 'Shanshui': Mountains and Water as Cosmic Balance

• Shanshui: Literally translates to 'Mountain' (Shan) and 'Water' (Shui).
• Mountain represents Yang: Solid, stable, vertical, and masculine.
• Water represents Yin: Fluid, moving, horizontal, and feminine.
• The goal of Shanshui is to depict the cosmic balance of Yin and Yang.
• Art as a living organism: Capturing 'Qi' (vital energy) rather than just form.
