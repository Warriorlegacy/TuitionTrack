Common Mistakes: Misinterpreting Character Motives and Contextual Errors

• Avoid labeling Grandfather as 'irresponsible'; use 'passionate animal lover' instead.
• Grandmother's reluctance is 'practicality,' not 'cruelty' or 'dislike.'
• Toto's actions are 'instinctive mischief,' not 'malicious intent.'
• Distinguish between a character's 'personality' and their 'motives.'
• The ending is a 'pragmatic realization,' not a 'defeat' by the animal.
