Literary Devices I: Metaphor, Simile, and Personification

• Personification: Giving human qualities to non-human elements like roots and leaves.
• Simile: A direct comparison between two things using 'like' or 'as'.
• Metaphor: An indirect comparison where one thing is described as being another.
• Extended Metaphor: The entire poem acts as a symbol for women's liberation.
• The 'House' vs. 'Forest' metaphor: Domestic restriction vs. social freedom.
