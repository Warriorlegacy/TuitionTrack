Common Mistakes: Avoiding Errors in Character Interpretation and Summary Writing

• Avoid labeling characters with simple adjectives; look for deeper symbolism.
• Distinguish between a character's intentions and their actual actions.
• Summary Writing: Focus on the narrative arc, not minor descriptive details.
• Maintain objectivity: Avoid using 'I think' or personal opinions in summaries.
• Use conjunctions to connect ideas instead of writing short, choppy sentences.
