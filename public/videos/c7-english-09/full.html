Common Mistakes: Avoiding Pitfalls in Interpretation and Grammar

• Avoid literalism: Distinguish between 'new' and 'well-maintained'.
• Use precise vocabulary: Move beyond simple emotions like 'happy' or 'sad'.
• Master the Literary Present Tense: Describe story events in the present.
• Subject-Verb Agreement: Ensure verbs match the true subject, not nearby nouns.
• Sentence Structure: Avoid run-on sentences by using correct punctuation.
