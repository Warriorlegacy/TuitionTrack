Transport Part 1: Land Transport - Roadways (Classification & Importance)

• Roads provide door-to-door service and feeder connectivity to other modes.
• Golden Quadrilateral: Connects Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, and Kolkata.
• National Highways: Maintained by Central Govt; connect state capitals.
• State Highways: Connect important cities within a state.
• Hierarchy: National > State > District > Rural > Other Roads.

Transport Part 2: Railways (Network, Challenges & Role in Economy)

• Railways: The backbone of long-distance transport and heavy freight
• Role: Facilitates commercial life and social integration across India
• Economic Impact: Drives industrial growth and creates massive employment

Transport Part 3: Waterways and Pipelines

• Waterways: Cheapest mode for heavy and bulky goods over long distances.
• Inland Waterways: Includes National Waterways like NW-1 (Ganga) and NW-2 (Brahmaputra).
• Oceanic Routes: Vital for international trade via India's 7,500 km coastline.
• Major Ports: Kandla, Mumbai, Chennai, and Visakhapatnam drive maritime commerce.
• Pipelines: Efficiently transport liquids and gases like crude oil and natural gas.

International Trade: Concept, Components, and Balance of Trade

• International Trade: Exchange of goods and services between nations
• Economic Barometer: Reflects the economic health of a country
• Exports: Goods produced domestically and sold abroad (Inflow of money)
• Imports: Goods bought from other countries (Outflow of money)
• Balance of Trade = Total Value of Exports minus Total Value of Imports
