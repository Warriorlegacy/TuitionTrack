• Distinguish between 'gloomy sadness' and 'peaceful solitude'.
• Recognize personification as a tool for showing nature's agency.

Writing Skills: How to Compose a Personal Diary Entry

• Format: Include Date, Day, and Time in the top left corner.
• Perspective: Always write in the First Person using 'I' and 'me'.
• Tone: Use an informal, conversational, and highly personal style.
• The Triple Formula: Describe the Event, your Feeling, and your Reflection.
• Depth: Avoid mere lists; focus on sensory details and emotional impact.
