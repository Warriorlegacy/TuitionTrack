The Medicinal Marvel: Scientific Properties & Benefits

• Key Compound: Azadirachtin acts as a natural insect repellent.
• Antibacterial & Antifungal: Nimbin and Nimbidin fight germs and skin infections.
• Oral Hygiene: Neem twigs help reduce plaque and prevent dental cavities.
• Immune Support: Acts as an immunomodulator to balance body defenses.
• Wound Healing: Antiseptic properties help clean and heal skin wounds.

Grammar Focus: Identifying Nouns and Adjectives in Text

• Noun: A naming word for people, places, animals, things, or ideas.

• Adjective: A describing word that modifies or adds detail to a noun.
