Common Mistakes: Avoiding Pitfalls in Interpretation and Grammar

• Avoid literal interpretations; look for symbolic meanings.
• Be precise with character emotions to avoid misattribution.
• Use the Literary Present Tense consistently in answers.
• Ensure Subject-Verb Agreement (e.g., 'The collection is...').
• Replace vague words like 'good' or 'bad' with precise vocabulary.
