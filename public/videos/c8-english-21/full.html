Literary Devices: Personification of Nature and Symbolism of Seasons

• Personification: Giving human traits to non-human elements like the North Wind.
• Nature as a Character: The weather reacts emotionally to the Giant's actions.
• Symbolism: Using seasons to represent deeper spiritual and emotional states.
• Spring = Rebirth: Symbolizes joy, life, and the presence of love.
• Winter = Isolation: Symbolizes the coldness and emptiness of a selfish heart.
