The Balancing Act: The Concept of LHS vs RHS and the Equality Principle

• An equation is a mathematical statement of equality.
• L-H-S stands for Left Hand Side; R-H-S stands for Right Hand Side.
• The equals sign (=) acts as the fulcrum of a mathematical scale.
• Equality Principle: Perform the same operation on both sides to maintain balance.
• Adding, subtracting, multiplying, or dividing must be done to both L-H-S and R-H-S.

Solving Equations: The Method of Transposition (Moving Terms Across the '=' Sign)

• Transposition means moving a term from one side of '=' to the other.
• Addition becomes Subtraction when moved across the '=' sign.
• Subtraction becomes Addition when moved across the '=' sign.
• Multiplication becomes Division when moved across the '=' sign.
• Division becomes Multiplication when moved across the '=' sign.

Solving Equations: The Method of Performing the Same Operation on Both Sides

• The Golden Rule: Whatever you do to one side, you must do to the other.
• Use Inverse Operations: Addition <-> Subtraction and Multiplication <-> Division.
• Goal: Isolate the variable on one side of the equals sign.
• Maintaining Balance: Performing the same operation keeps the equation true.
• Step-by-step: Identify operation, apply inverse to both sides, and simplify.

Common Pitfalls: Identifying and Avoiding Frequent Calculation and Sign Errors

• Sign Flip Error: Always change the sign when transposing terms.
• Inverse Operations: Use division for multiplication and addition for subtraction.
• Distributive Property: Multiply the outside term by every term inside the bracket.
• The Equality Principle: Apply operations to the entire side of the equation.
• Integer Accuracy: Double-check addition and subtraction of negative numbers.

Verification Techniques: How to Check Your Answer Using Substitution

• Verification means checking if your answer makes the equation true.
• Substitution: Replacing the variable with your calculated numerical value.
• Step 1: Calculate the Left Hand Side (LHS) using your answer.
• Step 2: Compare the result with the Right Hand Side (RHS).
• If LHS equals RHS, your solution is correct!
