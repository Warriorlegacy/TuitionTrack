Thematic Analysis: Materialism vs. Emotional Attachment

• Materialism: The view that objects are replaceable by money.
• Emotional Attachment: The deep, non-monetary bond with memories.
• The 'External' nature of money: It cannot buy back lost time or feelings.
• Value vs. Worth: The difference between price and sentimental importance.
• The Lesson: Learning to endure loss in a world of possessions.
