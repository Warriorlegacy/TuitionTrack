Grammar Focus: Understanding Tenses and Narrative Flow in the Story

• Simple Past: Completed actions (e.g., 'The travelers walked')
• Past Continuous: Ongoing background actions (e.g., 'The sun was shining')
• Past Perfect: Actions completed before another past action (e.g., 'had reached')
• Narrative Flow: Using time connectives like 'Suddenly' and 'Then'
• Tense Shifting: Moving from continuous to simple past to create tension
