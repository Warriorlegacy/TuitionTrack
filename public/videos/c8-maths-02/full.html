Understanding Linear Equations: Degree, Variables, and Equality

• Variable: A symbol like 'x' representing an unknown value.
• Equality: The '=' sign ensures both sides have the same value.
• Degree: The highest exponent of the variable in the equation.
• Linear Definition: An equation where the variable's degree is exactly one.
• Visualizing: Linear equations form straight lines on a graph.

Solving Equations: Transposition Method and Rules of Signs

• Goal: Isolate the variable on one side of the equation.
• Transposition: Moving terms across the '=' sign changes their operation.
• Addition becomes Subtraction; Subtraction becomes Addition.
• Multiplication becomes Division; Division becomes Multiplication.
• Crucial: Always apply the Rules of Signs when moving terms.

Solving Equations: Balancing Method and Step-by-Step Derivations

• The Equals Sign represents a perfectly balanced scale.
• Golden Rule: Perform the same operation on both sides.
• Inverse of Addition is Subtraction; Inverse of Subtraction is Addition.
• Inverse of Multiplication is Division; Inverse of Division is Multiplication.
• Isolating the variable means getting it alone on one side.

Part 6 of 12 · ~5 min

Equations with Variables on Both Sides: Systematic Reduction

• Goal: Isolate the variable on one side and constants on the other.
• Step 1: Use addition or subtraction to group all variable terms together.
• Step 2: Move all constant terms to the opposite side of the variable.
• Step 3: Use division to solve for the single variable.
• Golden Rule: Whatever operation is performed on one side must be done to the other.

Reducing Complex Fractions and Brackets in Linear Equations

• Use the Distributive Law to expand terms inside brackets.
• Multiply every term by the L.C.M. to eliminate denominators.
• Always apply operations to both sides to maintain balance.
• Simplify expressions before moving variables across the equals sign.
• Step 1: Remove brackets; Step 2: Clear fractions; Step 3: Solve.

Word Problems Part 1: Translating Statements into Mathematical Equations

• Identify the unknown value and assign it a variable like 'x'.
• Keywords: 'Is/Results in' means '=', 'Sum' means '+', 'Difference' means '-'.
• Watch out: 'Subtracted from' reverses the order of terms.
• 'Product' means multiplication and 'Quotient' means division.
• Break complex sentences into small, manageable mathematical chunks.

Word Problems Part 3: Perimeter, Ratio, and Currency-based Problems

Common Pitfalls: Identifying Errors in Signs, Brackets, and Operations

• Sign Error: Always flip the sign when transposing terms across the equals sign.
• Bracket Trap: Distribute the sign outside the bracket to EVERY term inside.
• Fractional Error: Multiply the entire numerator by the denominator to clear fractions.
• Variable Management: Keep variables on one side and constants on the other strictly.
• The Golden Rule: Always verify your final answer by substituting it back into the equation.
