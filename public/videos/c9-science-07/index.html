Topic 1 · Describing Motion: Distance and Displacement

Describing Motion: Distance and Displacement

Distance is the total path covered, while displacement is the shortest straight line between start and end. Think of distance as the winding road you drive and displacement as a bird's flight path.

• Distance is a scalar quantity (magnitude only).
• Displacement is a vector quantity (magnitude + direction).
• Displacement can be zero if you return to the starting point.

A runner goes 4m North then 3m East. Distance = 7m; Displacement = 5m (using Pythagoras).

Topic 2 · Uniform and Non-Uniform Motion

Uniform and Non-Uniform Motion

Uniform motion occurs when an object covers equal distances in equal intervals of time. Non-uniform motion is like a car in city traffic, speeding up and slowing down randomly.

• Uniform motion produces a straight line on a Distance-Time graph.
• Non-uniform motion produces a curved line on a Distance-Time graph.
• Speed = Distance / Time.

A cyclist covering 5m every second is uniform; covering 2m, then 5m, then 1m is non-uniform.

Topic 3 · Speed and Velocity

Speed and Velocity

Speed is how fast you move, but velocity is speed with a specific direction. It is like saying '50 km/h' (speed) versus '50 km/h towards North' (velocity).

• Average Speed = Total Distance / Total Time.
• Average Velocity = (Initial Velocity + Final Velocity) / 2.
• Velocity changes if either speed or direction changes.

A car travels 100km in 2h. Average speed = 100/2 = 50 km/h.

Topic 4 · Acceleration

Acceleration

Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity. It is like a runner's burst of speed at the start of a race to gain momentum.

• Formula: a = (v - u) / t.
• Positive acceleration means speeding up; negative (retardation) means slowing down.
• SI unit is m/s².

Velocity increases from 10 m/s to 30 m/s in 4s. a = (30-10)/4 = 5 m/s².

Topic 5 · Equations of Motion

Equations of Motion

These three mathematical formulas describe the relationship between distance, time, velocity, and acceleration for uniform acceleration.

• v = u + at
• s = ut + ½at²
• 2as = v² - u²

If u=0, a=2, t=3, find s: s = (0)(3) + ½(2)(3²) = 9m.
