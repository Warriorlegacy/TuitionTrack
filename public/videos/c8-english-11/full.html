Literary Devices: Rhyme Scheme, Rhythm, and Personification

• Rhyme Scheme: The pattern of sounds at the end of lines (e.g., A-B-A-B).
• Rhythm: The musical beat created by stressed and unstressed syllables.
• Personification: Giving human traits and emotions to animals or objects.
• Purpose: These devices turn a simple fable into a meaningful human allegory.
• Effect: Rhyme and rhythm create mood, while personification builds empathy.
