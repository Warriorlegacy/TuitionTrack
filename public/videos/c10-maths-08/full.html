The Foundation: Right-Angled Triangles, Perpendicular, Base, and Hypotenuse

• Right-angled triangle: One angle must be exactly 90 degrees.
• Hypotenuse: The longest side, always opposite the 90-degree angle.
• Perpendicular: The side opposite to the chosen acute angle (Theta).
• Base: The side adjacent to the chosen acute angle (Theta).
• Crucial Rule: Perpendicular and Base change depending on the angle chosen.

Defining the Ratios: Sine, Cosine, and Tangent (The SOH-CAH-TOA Concept)

• Sine (sin θ) = Perpendicular / Hypotenuse
• Cosine (cos θ) = Base / Hypotenuse
• Tangent (tan θ) = Perpendicular / Base
• Mnemonic: SOH-CAH-TOA
• tan θ = sin θ / cos θ

The Reciprocal Ratios: Cosecant, Secant, and Cotangent Explained

• Reciprocal means flipping a fraction: the reciprocal of a/b is b/a.
• Cosecant (cosec θ) is the reciprocal of Sine (1 / sin θ).
• Secant (sec θ) is the reciprocal of Cosine (1 / cos θ).
• Cotangent (cot θ) is the reciprocal of Tangent (1 / tan θ).
• Relationship: cot θ = cos θ / sin θ.

Trigonometric Ratios of Specific Angles: 0°, 30°, 45°, 60°, and 90°

• 45 degree triangle: Perpendicular equals Base
• 30 and 60 degree ratios derived from equilateral triangles
• Sine of 0 is 0; Sine of 90 is 1
• Cosine of 0 is 1; Cosine of 90 is 0
• Tangent of 90 degrees is undefined

Mastering the Trigonometric Table: Derivation, Memory Tricks, and Practice

• Derive Sine values using the pattern: square root of (0, 1, 2, 3, 4) divided by 2
• Cosine is the mirror image of the Sine row (reverse the order)
• Tangent is calculated as Sine divided by Cosine
• Reciprocal ratios: Cosecant is 1/Sine, Secant is 1/Cosine, Cotangent is 1/Tangent
• Practice Tip: Always simplify square roots and handle 'undefined' values carefully

Trigonometric Identities I: Proving and Deriving the Fundamental Identity (sin²θ + cos²θ = 1)

• Definition: An identity is an equation true for all values of the angle.
• The Foundation: Using Pythagoras Theorem (h² = p² + b²) to derive identities.
• Step 1: Express sin²θ as (p/h)² and cos²θ as (b/h)².
• Step 2: Combine fractions: (p² + b²) / h².
• Conclusion: Since p² + b² = h², the result is h²/h² = 1.
