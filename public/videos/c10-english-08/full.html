Common Mistakes: Avoiding Misinterpretations of Buddhist Philosophy and Textual Errors

• Avoid calling Buddhism 'pessimistic'; it is about realism and peace.
• Detachment is not 'lack of love,' but 'acceptance of impermanence.'
• The mustard seed was a teaching tool, not a 'trick' or 'test.'
• Use 'impermanence' instead of 'fate' to describe the cycle of life.
• Distinguish between Kisa Gotami's personal grief and universal truth.
