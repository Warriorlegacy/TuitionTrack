The Standard Meridian: Solving the Time Lag Problem

• Longitudinal extent of India is approximately thirty degrees.
• Earth rotates one degree every four minutes.
• The time lag between Arunachal Pradesh and Gujarat is two hours.
• Standard Meridian of India is 82 degrees 30 minutes East.
• Indian Standard Time (IST) is calculated based on Mirzapur, Uttar Pradesh.
