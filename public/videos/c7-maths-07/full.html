Concept 1: Ratio and Proportion - Definitions and Comparison

• Ratio: Comparing two quantities of the same kind using division.
• Crucial Rule: Quantities must be converted to the same units before comparing.
• Simplification: Always reduce ratios to their lowest terms.
• Proportion: A mathematical statement showing two ratios are equal.
• Property: Product of Extremes = Product of Means.

Concept 2: Unitary Method - Solving Problems Step-by-Step

• Unitary Method: Finding the value of a single unit first.
• Step 1: Divide to find the value of one unit.
• Step 2: Multiply to find the value of the required quantity.
• Many to One = Division | One to Many = Multiplication
• Always identify the 'given' and 'required' values first.

Concept 3: Percentage - Conversion and Basic Calculations

• Percentage means 'per hundred' or 'out of 100'
• Fraction to %: Multiply by 100 and add % symbol
• Decimal to %: Move decimal point two places to the right
• % to Fraction: Remove % and divide by 100, then simplify
• % to Decimal: Remove % and divide by 100 (move decimal left)

Concept 4: Finding Increase and Decrease Percentages

• Increase/Decrease is always calculated based on the Original Value.
• Step 1: Find the actual change (New Value - Original or vice versa).
• Step 2: Divide the change by the Original Value.
• Step 3: Multiply the result by 100 to get the percentage.
• Formula: (Change / Original Value) x 100

Concept 5: Profit and Loss - Basic Concepts and Formulas

• Cost Price (C-P): The total amount paid to purchase an item.
• Selling Price (S-P): The amount at which an item is sold.
• Profit = Selling Price - Cost Price (when S-P > C-P).
• Loss = Cost Price - Selling Price (when C-P > S-P).
• Profit/Loss % is always calculated on the Cost Price.

Part 7 of 12 · ~5 min

Concept 6: Profit and Loss - Advanced Word Problems and Cost Price/Selling Price

• Profit and Loss are always calculated on the Cost Price (CP)
• To find CP when Profit is given: CP = Selling Price - Profit
• To find CP when Loss is given: CP = Selling Price + Loss
• Profit Percentage is based on the original Cost Price
• Formula: CP = [SP × 100] / [100 + Profit%] or [100 - Loss%]

Concept 7: Simple Interest - Derivation and Formula Application

• Principal (P): The original amount borrowed or deposited.
• Rate (R): The percentage of interest charged per year.
• Time (T): The duration for which money is borrowed (in years).
• Simple Interest Formula: I = (P × R × T) / 100
• Total Amount: A = Principal + Interest

Class 7 Maths, Part 9: Interest, Principal, and Time - Solving for Unknown Variables

• Simple Interest Formula: I = (P × R × T) / 100
• To find Principal: P = (I × 100) / (R × T)
• To find Rate: R = (I × 100) / (P × T)
• To find Time: T = (I × 100) / (P × R)
• Crucial: Always convert Time into years before calculating

Concept 9: Common Mistakes and Conceptual Pitfalls in Quantities

• Always ensure units are identical before calculating ratios.
• Percentage change is always calculated on the original value.
• Profit and Loss percentages are based on the Cost Price.
• Time in Simple Interest formulas must always be in years.
• In percentage word problems, 'of' signifies multiplication.
