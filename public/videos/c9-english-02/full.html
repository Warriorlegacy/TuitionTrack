Bismillah Khan: Cultural Significance and the Journey from Pungi to Shehnai

• Aurangzeb banned the Pungi due to its shrill and unpleasant sound.
• The 'Nai' (barber) improved the instrument using a hollow stem and seven holes.
• The name 'Shehnai' comes from 'Shah' (Emperor) and 'Nai' (Barber).
• Shehnai became a 'Mangal Vadya', essential for weddings and temples.
• Bismillah Khan elevated the Shehnai from accompaniment to a solo classical instrument.
