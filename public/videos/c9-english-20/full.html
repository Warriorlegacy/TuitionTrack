Common Pitfalls: Avoiding Errors in Interpretation and Grammar

• Avoid misinterpreting the snake's intent as aggression; it is striving, not attacking.
• Maintain the Literary Present Tense when discussing poem events.
• Ensure Subject-Verb Agreement (e.g., 'The use of imagery creates...' not 'create').
• Replace vague adjectives like 'good' or 'bad' with precise terms like 'sinuous' or 'radiant'.
• Avoid over-generalizing; always link your arguments back to the specific text.
