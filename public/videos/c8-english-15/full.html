Poetic Structure: Rhyme Scheme, Meter, and Stanzaic Analysis of William Blake

• Rhyme Scheme: Predominantly A-B-A-B pattern creating a rhythmic, repetitive feel.
• Meter: Primarily Iambic meter, mimicking a heartbeat or steady pulse.
• Stanzaic Form: Six quatrains (four-line stanzas) providing a structured, rigid framework.
• Structural Irony: The disciplined form mirrors the restrictive school system described.
• Progression: Stanzas build an emotional arc from natural joy to systemic protest.

Literary Devices: Similes, Metaphors, Personification, and Alliteration

• Metaphor: A direct comparison stating one thing is another.
• Personification: Giving human qualities to non-human things.
