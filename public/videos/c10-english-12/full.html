Poetic Devices Masterclass: Simile, Metaphor, Personification, and Enjambment

• Simile: Comparison using 'like' or 'as' to create vivid imagery.
• Metaphor: Direct comparison of two unlike things for deeper meaning.
• Personification: Attributing human qualities (using 'He') to the tiger.
• Enjambment: Sentences continuing across lines without punctuation.
• Exam Tip: Always explain HOW the device enhances the poem's mood.

Common Mistakes: Misinterpreting Tone, Context, and Poetic Nuance

• Avoid shallow adjectives: Use 'suppressed rage' instead of just 'angry'.
• Understand the theme: It is a critique of captivity, not just a tiger description.
• Analyze word choice: 'Stalks' implies restricted, wasted predatory energy.
• Connect imagery: 'Brilliant eyes' vs 'Brilliant stars' signifies longing for freedom.
• Move from Literal to Literary: Describe the 'feeling' behind the physical actions.
