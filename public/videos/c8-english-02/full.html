Understanding the Tsunami: Definition, Causes, and Global Impact

• Tsunami: Japanese for 'Harbor Wave' (Tsu = Harbor, Nami = Wave)
• Cause: Sudden displacement of water, usually by undersea earthquakes
• Mechanism: Tectonic plate shifts release massive energy under the ocean
• Shoaling Effect: Waves grow taller and more destructive in shallow water
• Impact: Physical destruction, environmental damage, and loss of life

Grammar Focus: Understanding Tenses and Reported Speech in Narratives

• Past Continuous: was/were + verb-ing for background actions.
• Past Simple: V2 form for completed main events.
• Reported Speech: Shift tenses back (Present to Past).
• Pronoun and Time Changes: 'I' to 'he/she', 'now' to 'then'.
• Past Perfect: had + V3 for reporting past events.
