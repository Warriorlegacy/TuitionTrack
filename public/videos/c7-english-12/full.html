Literary Devices: Identifying Rhyme Scheme, Alliteration, and Metaphor

• Rhyme Scheme: The pattern of sounds at the end of lines (e.g., AABB).
• Alliteration: Repetition of consonant sounds at the start of nearby words.
• Metaphor: A direct comparison between two unlike things without using 'like' or 'as'.
• Purpose: Literary devices create rhythm, mood, and deeper meaning.
• Tip: Listen for the 'music' in the words to identify alliteration.

Conceptual Deep Dive: The Difference Between a Rebel and a Troublemaker
