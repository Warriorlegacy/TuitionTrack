Gender and Politics: Public/Private Division and Sexual Division of Labour

• Gender is a social construct, not just a biological difference.
• Public/Private Division: Women in the home, men in the public sphere.
• Sexual Division of Labour: Socially assigned roles based on gender.
• Domestic work is often unpaid and undervalued in economic terms.
• The division limits women's access to political decision-making.

• Low female representation in Lok Sabha and State Assemblies limits policy impact.
• Political representation is essential for addressing gender-specific issues.
• Feminist movements seek equality in both private and public spheres.
• One-third reservation for women exists in local government (Panchayats).
• The struggle continues for reservation in higher legislative bodies.

Religion, Communalism, and Politics: Defining Communalism and its Manifestations

• Communalism: Using religion to assert dominance over other groups.
• Core Idea: Assuming religious identity is the sole basis of social community.
• Manifestation 1: Everyday prejudices and religious stereotypes.
• Manifestation 2: Political mobilization using sacred symbols and leaders.
• Manifestation 3: Extreme forms like majoritarianism or demands for separatism.

A 3D flowchart showing 'Religion' splitting into 'Personal Faith' (peaceful) and 'Communalism' (a chain reaction leading to political mobilization and social conflict).

Secularism: The Constitutional Framework and Indian Model of Secularism

• Secularism: Separation of religion from state power
• No official state religion in the Indian Constitution
• Fundamental Rights: Articles 15 and 25-28 protect religious freedom
• Indian Model: Principled distance vs. Western strict separation
• State intervention is permitted to ensure social justice and equality

Caste and Politics: How Caste Influences Political Mobilization and Voting

• Caste influences politics through candidate selection based on constituency demographics.
• Political parties use caste-based mobilization to build large 'Vote Banks'.
• Caste mobilization can sometimes lead to social tension and communal conflict.
• Politics influences caste by encouraging groups to organize and form larger coalitions.
• The 'Politicization of Caste' transforms rigid hierarchies into fluid interest groups.

Politics in Caste: Caste Groups, Coalitions, and Political Identity

• Politics in Caste: A two-way relationship between social identity and power.
• Caste Expansion: Small groups merge to form larger, more powerful coalitions.
• New Identities: Emergence of political categories like 'Backward' and 'Forward'.
• Democratic Empowerment: Marginalized groups use caste to demand rights and resources.
• The Risk: Potential for social fragmentation and narrow identity-based politics.

Common Mistakes: Avoiding Conceptual Confusion in Social Science Answers

• Don't confuse Communalism (division) with Secularism (equality).
• Distinguish Sexual Division of Labour from Gender Inequality.
• Differentiate 'Caste in Politics' from 'Politics in Caste'.
• Use Intersectionality to show how identities overlap.
• Avoid absolute statements; use words like 'often' or 'tends to'.
