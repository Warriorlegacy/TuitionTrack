Comparative Study: Fog vs. Traditional Nature Poetry

• Sandburg's Approach: Minimalist, objective, and observational.

Common Mistakes: Misinterpreting Symbolism and Literalism

• Avoid Literalism: Don't treat the poem like a scientific weather report.
• Beware of Over-Symbolizing: Don't invent complex meanings not supported by text.
• The Missing Link: Always explain 'why' the cat is compared to the fog.
• Respect Brevity: Match your analytical style to the poem's concise nature.
• Acknowledge Agency: Treat the fog as an active character, not a passive event.
